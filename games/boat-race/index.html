BoatRace

  1. Each team has a boat.
  2. On your turn, answer a question. Right answer: your boat sails 1 step!
  3. Wrong answer? Your boat stays. Next team!
  4. Watch out for special tiles on the river. Each one works once for each team.
  5. The first boat to the finish line wins.
