Land Explorers

Grade 5 Math · Area & Length Units · m², hectares, acres, km², km

How to play

Teams take turns. Pick a country on the world map and answer the question. Right answer: the country is yours and you win its points. Wrong answer: the country stays free. When every country has an owner, the team with the most points wins. (Turn on Steal and Raid below for more action!)

Questions

✏️ Write my own questions

One question per line: question | right answer | wrong answer | wrong answer. You can add 1 to 3 wrong answers. One wrong answer (2 choices) = a ⭐ 100 question, more = ⭐ 200 and 300. Saved on this device only. Then pick “My questions” above.

Rules

Steal: if a team answers wrong, the next team can answer the same question and win the country for half the points.
Raid: a team may attack a country owned by another team with a harder question. Right: the country and its points change owner, and that country is locked 🔒. Wrong: the raiding team loses half the country's points.
Treasure: from round 3, a country that nobody owns gets a 💰 bonus at the end of every round (+50, up to +200). The team that wins the country takes the treasure too.

Rounds:

One round = every team plays one turn. The game ends after the last round.

📏 Unit Guide

Length: 1 km = 1,000 m
Area: 1 ha = 10,000 m²  ·  1 km² = 100 ha = 1,000,000 m²
Acre: 1 acre ≈ 4,047 m² ≈ 0.4 ha  ·  1 ha ≈ 2.5 acres (a hectare is bigger)
Area of a rectangle = length × width

🧑‍🏫 Teacher's tools

Too noisy (each time): 1st = yellow card (warning) · 2nd = −50 points · 3rd and more = −100 points. Quiet +50 = a reward for a calm, kind team.
