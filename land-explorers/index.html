Land Explorers

Grade 5 Math · Area & Length Units · m², hectares, acres, km², km

How to play

Teams take turns. Pick a place on the island and answer the question. Right answer: the land is yours and you win its points. Wrong answer: the land stays free. When every place has an owner, the team with the most points wins. (Turn on Steal and Raid below for more action!)

Rules

Steal: if a team answers wrong, the next team can answer the same question and win the land for half the points.
Raid: a team may attack land owned by another team with a harder question. Right: the land and its points change owner, and that land is locked 🔒. Wrong: the raiding team loses half the land's points.

Rounds:

One round = every team plays one turn. The game ends after the last round.

📏 Unit Guide

Length: 1 km = 1,000 m
Area: 1 ha = 10,000 m²  ·  1 km² = 100 ha = 1,000,000 m²
Acre: 1 acre ≈ 4,047 m² ≈ 0.4 ha  ·  1 ha ≈ 2.5 acres (a hectare is bigger)
Area of a rectangle = length × width
